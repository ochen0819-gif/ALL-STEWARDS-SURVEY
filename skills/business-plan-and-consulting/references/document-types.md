# Document types: what each one must contain, in what order, at what length

Pick the type the reader expects, then build the storyline (`storyline.md`)
inside that skeleton. The skeleton is a container, not a template to fill
evenly: a section the reader does not need gets one line or is cut.

Contents
1. Business plan, investor version (中文 BP / English)
2. Business plan, government, park or bank version (申报 / 贷款)
3. Pitch deck (seed to Series B)
4. Executive summary and one-page teaser
5. Investment memo (for an investment committee or a lead investor)
6. Consulting report and steering-committee deck
7. CIM / information memorandum and bank pitch book
8. IPO prospectus summary (S-1 / HKEX / A股招股说明书概览)
9. Narrative memo (board, six-pager)
10. Length and reading-time norms

---

## 1. Business plan, investor version

Reader: a fund partner or family office principal who gives it 10 to 15
minutes and decides whether to take a meeting. The plan answers four things:
team, market space, product, how it makes money. Everything else supports
those four.

Order (Word 15 to 30 pages, or a deck of 15 to 20 pages):

1. 执行摘要 / Executive summary, one page. The company in one declarative
   sentence (for whom, what problem, how it earns). The three strongest
   numbers. The ask: amount, what it buys, by when. Written last.
2. 市场与客户 / Market and customer. Named buyer, quantified pain, current
   alternatives. Market built bottom-up (customers x adoption x price) with
   the arithmetic shown; a top-down figure only as a cross-check. Beachhead
   first, expansion path second. Why now: the inflection with a date.
3. 产品与服务 / Product. What exists today, not the roadmap. Three benefits or
   three steps. One screenshot or photo per page at most. Barriers: switching
   cost, data, licences, distribution, relationships. Features are not moats.
4. 商业模式与单位经济 / Business model. One primary model. Pricing, take rate
   or ARPU, gross margin, CAC and payback by channel. See `financials.md`.
5. 进展与里程碑 / Traction and milestones. The single strongest metric with
   its trend and period; signed contracts beat pilots beat letters of intent.
   The next 12 to 24 months as dated, measurable milestones.
6. 竞争 / Competition. Named competitors and the do-nothing alternative. A
   matrix on the axes customers actually buy on, plus one sentence on why we
   win. "No competitors" is never written.
7. 团队 / Team. Founder-market fit as evidence: years in the domain, P&L run,
   exits, projects delivered, time working together. Gaps and the hiring
   plan. No advisor wall.
8. 财务预测 / Financials. Three-year summary P&L, the key-assumptions table
   it is driven by, current cash, burn and runway. Downside case.
9. 融资计划 / The ask. One number. Use of funds in money and percent by
   category, with headcount and timing. Milestones the money buys, with
   baseline and target. Runway after the round. 拟出让比例或估值区间 is
   customary in Chinese BPs; omit valuation in US-style decks.
10. 风险 / Risks. Three to five that could break the thesis, each with the
    evidence, the mitigation and who owns it. "The team needs to execute" is
    not a risk.
11. 附录 / Appendix. Model, cohorts, pipeline, certificates, patents, the
    hard questions answered as an FAQ.

Exit mechanisms (IPO, M&A, buyback) belong in the Chinese investor version
and in the appendix of an English one; never on a seed deck.

## 2. Business plan, government, park or bank version

Readers here score against a form. Keep the form's headings verbatim and put
the judgment inside them.

申报 / 产业基金 (参照沪京深资金申请报告提纲) adds: 项目背景与必要性（与产业政策、园区定位的契合）；申报单位基本情况（注册、股东、信用、审计报表）；建设内容与实施方案；总投资估算（设备、工程、流动资金分项）与资金筹措（自有资金证明、银行贷款、政府资金介入方式，资金闭环）；用地与选址（权属、供地方式）；进度与考核指标；经济效益（IRR、投资利润率、回收期、税收）；社会效益（就业、产业带动、绿色）；风险分析。

银行贷款版 leads with repayment: 借款人资信与报表；还款来源与现金流覆盖；抵押与担保；用途合规；流动资金测算；偿债指标（资产负债率、流动比率、利息保障倍数）。

## 3. Pitch deck

Ten to fourteen core slides plus an appendix. Average investor read time is
under three minutes and about 15 seconds per slide after the first, so the
title is the conclusion and each slide carries one number.

Sequoia order with its own guidance: company purpose (one declarative
sentence); problem (the pain, how it is handled today, why that falls
short); solution (the key insight, why it endures); why now (what changed:
technology, regulation, behaviour); market (identify the customer; the best
companies create their market); competition (direct and indirect, a credible
plan to win); business model; team; financials; vision (five years out).

YC adds: traction right after solution if it is strong; the ask as the last
slide with amount, use and milestones, without valuation. CRV: lead with the
strongest asset. Reid Hoffman: open with the investment thesis (the three to
eight things an investor must believe), confront the top objections in the
first few slides, show delivery against the previous round's promises, end
on the thesis slide and leave it up for questions. Never a "Thank you" slide.

Most-read slides are team, financials and traction. Product gets the least
time, so one product slide is enough.

Lessons from named decks: Airbnb showed the revenue arithmetic (trips x
share x fee) and one milestone per dollar; its weaknesses were label
headlines and copyable "advantages". LinkedIn's Series B listed three
revenue models, which Hoffman now says to avoid. Front's Series A won on
capital efficiency, cohort retention with candid commentary and named
acquisition channels; it lost points for no use-of-funds model and a thin
team slide. Uber's 2008 deck had no spine because product slides were
scattered and traction came last.

## 4. Executive summary and teaser

Executive summary spec is in `storyline.md` section 3. A one-page teaser for
first contact: project or company name; one paragraph on the business
(industry, geography, size, model); four to seven investment highlights,
each a sentence with a number; a financial snapshot table (revenue, gross
profit, EBITDA for three historical and one or two forecast years); what is
offered and the next step; contact. One page, two at most.

## 5. Investment memo

For a committee, or written on behalf of a founder to a lead investor who
must sell the deal internally. Three to six pages for seed or A, up to 20
with appendices at growth stage. The Sequoia YouTube memo and the Bessemer
Shopify memo are the public benchmarks; both are short, numeric and put
detail in the appendix.

1. Decision snapshot: company, round, instrument, cheque, ownership,
   recommendation, confidence. One page.
2. Thesis in three to five sentences: we believe X because Y; if true the
   company is worth Z.
3. Company, problem, customer: buyer versus user, cost of the pain, current
   alternatives, why now.
4. Product and differentiation: what exists today, why it is hard to copy.
5. Market and competition: narrow initial segment, bottom-up size,
   substitutes including do nothing, expansion path.
6. Model and go-to-market: pricing, variable cost, sales cycle, what has
   repeated.
7. Traction and evidence: trend with period and source; reference calls
   including the disagreements; what is still unknown.
8. Team: founder-market fit, record, time together, gaps and hires.
9. Cash, burn, runway before and after; the milestone the round buys;
   stress case.
10. Cap table and terms: instruments outstanding, dilution, rights.
11. Key risks: three to five that could break the thesis, each with failure
    mode, evidence, likelihood, impact, mitigation and the decision trigger.
12. What has to be true: downside, base and upside with shared assumptions;
    ownership at exit x exit value against fund size.
13. Recommendation: decision, conditions, the two decisive reasons, the
    strongest counter-argument, the next review trigger.
14. Sources: verified facts separated from founder claims and assumptions.

## 6. Consulting report and steering-committee deck

Report (Word or PDF): 封面（中英文标题、日期、机构、保密级别如需要）→ 摘要一页（结论先行，3 到 5 条带数字判断，决策请求）→ 目录（超过 10 页才要）→ 背景与问题定义（S+C，1 到 2 页）→ 核心发现（每章一个判断句标题，每页一个信息，图表证明）→ 建议与路线图（动词开头，责任人、时间、投入、收益）→ 风险与应对 → 附录（方法、数据、访谈名单）。

Steering-committee or client-update deck, six to twelve pages: answer slide
(on or off track, the one condition that decides it); the two or three
drivers each on its own page with the evidence; decisions requested with
owner and date; next 30 days; appendix with the detail. Trackers between
sections only when the deck exceeds about 15 pages.

Public-report conventions worth copying: exhibits numbered continuously
(Exhibit 1, 2 … or 图 1, 2 …), titled with a sentence, unit line under the
title, then numbered footnotes, then "Source: … ; [firm] analysis" with the
firm's own work last; a technical appendix instead of method in the body; a
signed foreword only in a published piece, never in a client deliverable.

## 7. CIM and bank pitch book

CIM, 40 to 80 pages after NDA: executive summary and investment highlights
(five to ten pages, where senior time goes); transaction overview; company
overview; industry; products; customers and sales; operations; management;
financial overview (three to five years historical, bridge to adjusted
EBITDA, projections with assumptions, capex, working capital, cash
conversion); growth strategy, each lever quantified; risk factors (required
in a debt CIM); appendices.

Investment highlights: four to eight, each a complete sentence with a number,
most provable first. Buyers sort them into "proved by the numbers later" and
"merely asserted"; the second kind costs credibility. Buyers skim the
executive summary for fit, jump to the financials, then the market. If the
deal does not work on management's numbers it will not work on realistic
ones.

Credibility failures: two years of history when year three was weak;
re-bid contracts shown as locked in; "stable" for flat; discretionary
add-backs; projections above the run rate; typos.

Pitch book: situation and company overview; industry; valuation (football
field with one colour per method, implied range to two decimals, current
price as a vertical line; trading comps; precedents; DCF); strategic
alternatives with a recommendation; team and tombstones; appendix. Every page
has an action title, page number, confidentiality line, Source and Note.

## 8. IPO prospectus summary

The skill does not draft a full prospectus. It drafts or sharpens the
Summary / 概览 and the Business or Risk sections, and checks that the plain
language rules are met.

US S-1 order: cover; prospectus summary (overview, strengths, strategy,
summary of risk factors, the offering table, summary financial data); risk
factors in order of importance, specific subheadings, no mitigating
language, no generic risks; forward-looking statements; use of proceeds;
dividend policy; capitalization; dilution; MD&A; business; management;
compensation; related-party transactions; principal stockholders; capital
stock; shares eligible for future sale; tax; underwriting; experts; F-pages.
SEC Rule 421(d) for the cover, summary and risk factors: short sentences;
concrete everyday words; active voice; tables and bullets for complex
material; no legal jargon; no multiple negatives. Charts drawn to scale.

HKEX (GL86-16): Summary ≤ 10 pages, Industry Overview ≤ 10, History ≤ 20,
Laws and Regulations ≤ 20; vetting is suspended if the Summary is copied from
elsewhere or omits the explanation of material fluctuations. Summary must
carry: business model with a revenue flow diagram; major stakeholders (top
customers, suppliers, financiers and relationships); market position with
share or ranking, and a segment claim must state the segment's share of the
whole industry; strengths only if genuinely unique, no marketing
statements; shareholders and pre-IPO investors; key operational and
financial data on one page; recent developments to the latest practicable
date; offering statistics, use of proceeds at low, mid and high price, dividend
policy, listing expenses, risk highlights. One risk per risk factor, ordered
by materiality, no mitigants inside a risk factor.

A股《准则第57号》: 第八条语言规则：事实描述性语言，不得使用市场推广和宣传用语；直接、简洁、确定的语句，日常用语、短句和图表，避免公文用语，不得从法律文件摘抄而不解释；不得简单罗列堆砌，避免模板化。概览按重要性排序并声明"本概览仅对招股说明书全文作扼要提示"：重大事项提示；发行人及中介机构基本情况表；本次发行概况表；主营业务概述（产品、原材料与供应商、生产模式、销售渠道与客户、竞争地位的份额或排名）；板块定位；报告期主要财务数据和指标表；审计截止日后经营状况；上市标准；治理特殊安排；募集资金运用；其他重大事项。风险因素：一项风险因素不得描述多个风险，须定量并作敏感性分析，不得包含风险对策或竞争优势表述。募集资金：投向、与主业和核心技术的关系、缺口来源、偿债用途的金额利率到期日。

## 9. Narrative memo

Board paper, investment-committee paper or Amazon-style six-pager. Six pages
of prose, no bullets in the body, appendices unlimited. Decision sought in
the first paragraph; goals; tenets; state of the business with numbers;
lessons learned; the plan; FAQ with the hardest objections answered;
appendix. See `storyline.md` section 8.

## 10. Length and reading-time norms

| Document | Length | Reader's time |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser / one-pager | 1 page | 3 minutes |
| Executive summary | 1 page, 300 to 500 words | 2 minutes |
| Pitch deck | 10 to 14 core slides + appendix | under 3 minutes unattended, 20 minutes presented |
| Investor business plan | 15 to 30 pages Word or 15 to 20 slides | 15 minutes |
| Investment memo | 3 to 6 pages (seed/A), 10 to 20 (growth) | 20 minutes |
| Steering-committee deck | 6 to 12 pages + appendix | 30-minute meeting |
| Consulting report | 20 to 60 pages, summary stands alone | summary read, body consulted |
| CIM | 40 to 80 pages | 15-minute skim, then financials |
| Six-pager | 6 pages + appendix | 20 to 30 minutes silent reading |
| 政府申报报告 | per the form | scored against the form |
